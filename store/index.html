Our approach

Made in Britain, chosen slowly.

We work with a small number of makers — a candle studio, a Yorkshire woollen mill, a pottery in Stoke-on-Trent — and put our name only on things we would keep ourselves.

No seasonal churn, no invented claims. A short catalogue that we intend to still be selling in five years.
