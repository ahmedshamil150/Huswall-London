Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar browser storage used on the Hasswell London website, what they do, and how you can control them.

What we use

Name Type Purpose Duration
hl_cart_v1 Essential (local storage) Keeps the items in your bag between pages and visits. Until cleared
sb-*-auth-token Essential (local storage) Keeps you signed in to your account. Set by our authentication provider. Session / refresh cycle
hl_checkout_key Essential (session storage) Prevents a duplicate order if you refresh during checkout. Browser session
_ga, _ga_* Analytics (only if configured) Google Analytics 4, used to understand which pages are used. IP anonymisation is enabled. Up to 24 months
Payment provider cookies Essential (payment page) Set only on the hosted payment page when card payment is enabled. Used for fraud prevention. Set by provider

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is loaded only when a measurement ID has been configured for the store. It is used in aggregate to understand page usage. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not enable Google Signals.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage will stop the bag and sign-in from working. Because analytics is loaded only where configured, you can also use a browser content blocker to prevent it.

For more information about cookies and your choices, see the Information Commissioner's Office guidance at ico.org.uk.
