Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Hasswell London collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and the rights you have under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who is responsible for your data

Hasswell London is the data controller for the personal data described here. Our contact address for privacy matters is hello@hasswelllondon.co.uk. Postal address: London, United Kingdom.

Company registration and ICO registration details are available on request.

What we collect

  • Order data: name, email address, phone number, delivery address, items ordered, order value and order history.
  • Account data: email address, password (stored only as a hash by our authentication provider), name and phone number.
  • Payment data: the payment provider's transaction reference and the result of the payment. We do not receive or store card numbers.
  • Correspondence: messages you send through the contact form or by email.
  • Marketing data: your email address and subscription status, if you choose to subscribe.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages viewed and approximate location. IP addresses used for rate limiting are hashed.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To fulfil your order (contract): taking payment, packing and dispatching goods, providing tracking and handling returns.
  • To manage your account (contract): authenticating you and showing your order history.
  • To answer your messages (legitimate interests): responding to enquiries and resolving problems.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse (legitimate interests and legal obligation): rate limiting, validating orders and keeping transaction records.
  • To send marketing emails (consent): only if you subscribe, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve the website (legitimate interests, or consent where required): aggregated analytics.
  • To meet legal obligations (legal obligation): tax, accounting and consumer law record keeping.

Who we share data with

We share only what is necessary with:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and image storage.
  • Vercel — website hosting and serverless functions.
  • Our payment provider — to process your payment. The active provider is shown at checkout.
  • Our fulfilment and supplier partners — name, delivery address, phone number and order contents, so your order can be packed and delivered.
  • Our email provider — to send order confirmations, dispatch notices and, if subscribed, marketing emails.
  • Couriers — name, delivery address and phone number for delivery and tracking.
  • Professional advisers and authorities — where required by law or to establish or defend legal claims.

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where that happens, transfers rely on the UK's adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Addendum / Standard Contractual Clauses with appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

  • Order and transaction records: six years from the end of the relevant financial year, for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Account data: while your account is open, then deleted or anonymised on request.
  • Contact messages: up to two years after the matter is resolved.
  • Marketing subscriptions: until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record so we do not contact you again.
  • Server logs and rate-limit records: short retention periods, typically 30 days or less.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access a copy of your personal data;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • erase data where there is no lawful reason for us to keep it;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent for marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@hasswelllondon.co.uk. We will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and local storage

We use essential browser storage to keep your bag and sign-in session working, and optional analytics only where configured. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to customer and order data is restricted by database row-level security and administrative access controls. Card data never reaches our servers. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the ICO where required.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services or the law. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
