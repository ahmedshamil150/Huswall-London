Delivery & Returns

The practical details: how orders are sent, what happens if something is wrong, and how to return an item.

Delivery

We currently deliver to addresses within the United Kingdom. The available delivery options and their charges are shown at checkout before you pay. We do not add charges after your order is placed.

Orders are packed and dispatched by our fulfilment partners. Once dispatch is confirmed, we send you a tracking number by email and show it in your account. Tracking can take a short time to become active with the courier after a parcel is collected.

If your order contains more than one item, it may arrive in separate parcels. You will not be charged extra for split deliveries.

When your order is sent

We do not advertise a guaranteed dispatch time on this page because it depends on the items ordered and the fulfilment partner handling them. Any timeframe shown at checkout or in your confirmation email is the one that applies to your order.

If an item becomes unavailable after you have paid, we will contact you and refund that item in full. We will never substitute a different product without your agreement.

Returns

If you are a consumer in the United Kingdom, you have a statutory right to cancel an online order within 14 days of receiving the goods, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. You then have a further 14 days to return the goods to us. This is in addition to our own returns window below.

Our own policy allows returns within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, in its original packaging and in a resaleable condition. This does not affect your statutory rights.

Items we cannot accept for a change-of-mind return

  • Opened or used bath, body and beauty products, for hygiene reasons.
  • Perishable goods such as tea once the seal has been broken.
  • Personalised or made-to-order items, unless faulty.
  • Gift cards.

How to return an item

  1. Email hello@hasswelllondon.co.uk with your order number and the item you wish to return.
  2. We will reply with return instructions and the correct address.
  3. Pack the item securely and send it back within 14 days of our reply. Please keep proof of postage.
  4. Once we receive and check the item, we will refund the price you paid for it within 14 days. We do not deduct a restocking fee.

Unless the item is faulty or was sent in error, the cost of returning it is yours. If the item is faulty, we will cover reasonable return postage — please contact us before sending anything back.

Faulty or incorrect items

If something arrives damaged, faulty or is not what you ordered, contact us within 30 days with your order number and a photograph where possible. We will arrange a replacement or a refund, including any delivery charge you paid. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

Do not send a damaged item back before speaking to us — we may not need it returned, and we cannot reimburse postage that was not agreed in advance.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order before it is dispatched by emailing us as soon as possible. If the order has already left our fulfilment partner, please use the returns process instead.

Refunds

Refunds are made to the original payment method. Your bank or card provider may take a few working days to show the credit after we have issued it. We will confirm by email when the refund has been sent.
