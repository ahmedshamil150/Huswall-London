Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, write to hello@hasswelllondon.co.uk.

Ordering

Do I need an account to order?
No. You can check out as a guest. Creating an account lets you see your order history in one place, but it is optional.
How do I know an item is in stock?
Each product page shows live availability. If an item is out of stock it is marked as sold out and cannot be added to your bag. Stock is reserved when you place an order and confirmed when payment is verified, so the same item cannot be sold twice.
Can I change my order after placing it?
Contact us as soon as possible. We can usually change or cancel an order before it is passed to our fulfilment partner. Once it is being packed, it may be too late; in that case you can use the returns process.
Why was my payment declined?
Payment authorisation is handled by your bank and our payment provider, not by us, so we cannot see the reason for a decline. Check your card details and try again, or use a different payment method if one is offered. If your order was created but payment did not complete, it will be shown as pending and will not be dispatched.

Delivery

Where do you deliver?
We currently deliver within the United Kingdom. The exact countries we serve are shown at checkout. If your country is not listed, we cannot currently send an order there.
How much is delivery?
The delivery charge is shown at checkout before you pay, based on the current store settings and the contents of your bag. We do not add any charge after you have paid.
Where is my tracking number?
Tracking is issued when your parcel is dispatched. It appears in your account and in your dispatch email. Tracking can take a short time to activate with the courier after collection.
My parcel has not arrived. What should I do?
Check the tracking link first. If it has not moved for several working days, or the courier says it was delivered and it was not, contact us with your order number and we will investigate with the courier.

Returns and refunds

What is your returns policy?
You can return unused items within 30 days of delivery for a refund. This is in addition to your statutory 14-day right to cancel. Full details, including exclusions, are on the Delivery & Returns page.
How long does a refund take?
We issue refunds within 14 days of receiving and checking the returned item. Your bank may take a few further working days to show the credit. We email you when the refund has been sent.
Can I return a beauty product?
For hygiene reasons we cannot accept opened or used bath and body products for a change-of-mind return, unless they are faulty. Unopened products in their original packaging can be returned within the policy window.

Products

Are your candles and soaps tested on animals?
Our own-brand bath and body products are not tested on animals. Where a product is made by a partner workshop, the product page states the relevant information. If it is not stated and you need it, ask us before ordering.
How should I care for my wool throw or linen cushion?
Care instructions are enclosed with each order and repeated on the product page where relevant. As a rule, wool should be aired rather than washed often, and linen is happiest washed cool and line dried. Contact us if you have lost the care card.
Do you offer gift wrapping?
Orders are packed in recycled board that is suitable for gifting, with no prices included. If you need a handwritten note, ask us before ordering and we will confirm whether it is possible for that item.

Account and privacy

How do I delete my account?
Email us from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will remove your profile and marketing preferences. We may need to retain order records for accounting and legal purposes, as explained in our Privacy Policy.
Do you store my card details?
No. Card details are entered on our payment provider's secure page and are never sent to or stored by our servers.
