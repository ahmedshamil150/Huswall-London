About Hasswell London

A short catalogue, chosen slowly.

We make and curate objects for the home with the intention that they are still in use long after the season they were bought in.

The idea

Fewer things, better made.

Hasswell London began with a simple frustration: it is difficult to buy a plain, well-made object without also buying a story about it. We wanted a shop that sold the object and left the story to the making.

So the catalogue is deliberately short. A candle that burns cleanly and evenly. A throw with enough weight to be useful. A wash that is pleasant to use every day. Each piece is made in small batches by a workshop we have spoken to, and each is expected to improve with handling.

We design in London and produce with British makers wherever the craft exists here. Where it does not, we say so plainly and choose the smallest responsible workshop we can find.

How we work

Three commitments we can actually keep.

01

Materials we can name

British wool, European flax, olive and coconut oils, cold-pressed bergamot. If we cannot name an ingredient, it does not go in.

02

Makers we speak to

We work directly with the studios that make our pieces. No anonymous supply chains, no white-label ranges with our name added later.

03

Claims we can support

No invented heritage, no unverified reviews, no delivery promises we cannot keep. What is on the page is what we know to be true.

Packaging

Orders are packed in recycled board with paper tape. We do not use plastic void fill. If a piece needs protection in transit, we use a recyclable paper wrap.

Stock and availability

Availability shown on each product page is live. If something sells out, it is marked as such rather than left available to order.
